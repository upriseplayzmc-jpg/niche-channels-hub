Privacy policy: Niche Channels Hub

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Niche Channels Hub ("the app") is a private tool used by one person to manage their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app can access, for the channels the owner chooses:

  • the channel's basic details (name, ID) and its videos, playlists and thumbnails;
  • YouTube Analytics and Reporting data for those channels: views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, impressions and, once monetized, revenue.

The app does not access anyone else's channels, and it does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube Analytics reports to the channel owner.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's approved videos and to produce the owner's own performance reports.

Where it is stored and for how long

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server. Statistics are kept for as long as the owner keeps running the channels. Tokens are deleted when a channel is disconnected.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
